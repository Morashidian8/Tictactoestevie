# Before you post a generated video: disclosure and rights checklist

Six checks for AI-generated clips before they go on TikTok, Reels, Shorts or a client's site: labels, likeness, music, logos, model terms and provenance.

Category: generation · difficulty: beginner · time: 5 min · works with: manual · verified: False
Source: https://superduperai.co/library/ai-video-disclosure-and-rights-checklist/

## Prompt / recipe

```
Before posting a generated clip, answer each in writing:
1. LABEL — Does the platform require an AI-generated / altered-content label for this kind of content (realistic people, events, places)? Set it in the upload form, not only in the caption.
2. LIKENESS — Does anyone in the clip resemble a real, identifiable person who did not consent? If yes, regenerate with the character block changed.
3. MUSIC — Was the music generated, licensed, or from the platform's library? Anything else gets stripped before upload.
4. LOGOS & BRANDS — Any third-party logo, trademark or recognisable product in frame that you do not own? Prompt 'no logos' and check the frames.
5. MODEL TERMS — Does your plan on {Veo / Sora / Kling / Runway} allow commercial use of outputs? Keep a screenshot of the terms page with the date.
6. PROVENANCE — Keep the prompt, the seed if shown, the model name and the date beside the clip (a sidecar text file). It is your proof of how it was made.
```

## Tips

- Platform label rules change several times a year; the check is 'did I look at today's upload form', not 'what was true last spring'.
- The sidecar file costs 10 seconds and answers every 'how was this made' question later.
